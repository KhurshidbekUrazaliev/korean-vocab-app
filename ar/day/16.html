Day 16: Directions الِاتِّجَاهَات

Learn 10 Arabic words about directions: مَشَى (to walk), قَادَ (to drive), اِنْعَطَفَ (to turn), الْيَمِين (right) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. مَشَى (mashaa): to walk
  2. قَادَ (qaada): to drive
  3. اِنْعَطَفَ (in‘atafa): to turn
  4. الْيَمِين (al-yamiin): right
  5. الْيَسَار (al-yasaar): left
  6. إِلَى الْأَمَام (ilaa al-amaam): straight ahead, forward
  7. قَرِيب (qariib): near
  8. بَعِيد (ba‘iid): far
  9. الْخَرِيطَة (al-khariita): map
  10. الْإِشَارَة (al-ishaara): sign, signal
