Day 27: Eating out فِي الْمَطْعَم

Learn 10 Arabic words about eating out: قَائِمَةُ الطَّعَام (menu), الْفَاتُورَة (bill), الْبَخْشِيش (tip), طَلَبَ (to order) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. قَائِمَةُ الطَّعَام (qaa’imat at-ta‘aam): menu
  2. الْفَاتُورَة (al-faatuura): bill
  3. الْبَخْشِيش (al-bakhshiish): tip
  4. طَلَبَ (talaba): to order
  5. النَّادِل (an-naadil): waiter
  6. الْمِنْدِيل (al-mindiil): napkin
  7. جَائِع (jaa’i‘): hungry
  8. عَطْشَان (‘atshaan): thirsty
  9. لَذِيذ (ladhiidh): delicious
  10. نَبَاتِيّ (nabaatii): vegetarian
