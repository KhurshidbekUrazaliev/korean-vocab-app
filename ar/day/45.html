Day 45: Sport الرِّيَاضَة

Learn 10 Arabic words about sport: كُرَةُ الْقَدَم (football), كُرَةُ السَّلَّة (basketball), الْفَرِيق (team), الْمُبَارَاة (match) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. كُرَةُ الْقَدَم (kurat al-qadam): football
  2. كُرَةُ السَّلَّة (kurat as-salla): basketball
  3. الْفَرِيق (al-fariiq): team
  4. الْمُبَارَاة (al-mubaaraa): match
  5. الْمَلْعَب (al-mal‘ab): pitch, stadium
  6. اللَّاعِب (al-laa‘ib): player
  7. الْهَدَف (al-hadaf): goal
  8. الْحَكَم (al-hakam): referee
  9. رَبِحَ (rabiha): to win
  10. خَسِرَ (khasira): to lose
