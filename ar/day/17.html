Day 17: The house الْبَيْت

Learn 10 Arabic words about the house: الْبَيْت (house), الشَّقَّة (flat), الْغُرْفَة (room), الْبَاب (door) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْبَيْت (al-bayt): house, home
  2. الشَّقَّة (ash-shaqqa): flat, apartment
  3. الْغُرْفَة (al-ghurfa): room
  4. الْبَاب (al-baab): door
  5. النَّافِذَة (an-naafidha): window
  6. الْحَائِط (al-haa’it): wall
  7. السَّقْف (as-saqf): ceiling, roof
  8. الْأَرْضِيَّة (al-ardiyya): floor
  9. الدَّرَج (ad-daraj): stairs
  10. الْمِفْتَاح (al-miftaah): key
