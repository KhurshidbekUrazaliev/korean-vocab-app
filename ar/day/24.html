Day 24: Vegetables الْخُضْرَوَات

Learn 10 Arabic words about vegetables: الطَّمَاطِم (tomatoes), الْبَطَاطَا (potatoes), الْخِيَار (cucumber), الْجَزَر (carrots) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الطَّمَاطِم (at-tamaatim): tomatoes
  2. الْبَطَاطَا (al-bataataa): potatoes
  3. الْخِيَار (al-khiyaar): cucumber
  4. الْجَزَر (al-jazar): carrots
  5. الْبَصَل (al-basal): onions
  6. الثُّوم (ath-thuum): garlic
  7. الْخَسّ (al-khass): lettuce
  8. الْبَاذِنْجَان (al-baadhinjaan): aubergine, eggplant
  9. الْفُلْفُل (al-fulful): pepper
  10. الْفُطْر (al-futr): mushrooms
