Day 37: More of the body أَعْضَاءُ الْجِسْم

Learn 10 Arabic words about more of the body: الْعُنُق (neck), الْكَتِف (shoulder), الظَّهْر (back), الْبَطْن (abdomen) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْعُنُق (al-‘unuq): neck
  2. الْكَتِف (al-katif): shoulder
  3. الظَّهْر (adh-dhahr): back
  4. الْبَطْن (al-batn): abdomen, belly
  5. الصَّدْر (as-sadr): chest
  6. الْإِصْبَع (al-isba‘): finger
  7. الْقَدَم (al-qadam): foot
  8. الرُّكْبَة (ar-rukba): knee
  9. الْأَسْنَان (al-asnaan): teeth
  10. اللِّسَان (al-lisaan): tongue
