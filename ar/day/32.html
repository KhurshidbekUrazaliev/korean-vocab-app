Day 32: Education التَّعْلِيم

Learn 10 Arabic words about education: الطَّالِب (student), الدَّرْس (lesson), الِامْتِحَان (exam), الْوَاجِبُ الْمَنْزِلِيّ (homework) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الطَّالِب (at-taalib): student
  2. الدَّرْس (ad-dars): lesson
  3. الِامْتِحَان (al-imtihaan): exam
  4. الْوَاجِبُ الْمَنْزِلِيّ (al-waajib al-manzilii): homework
  5. الْجَامِعَة (al-jaami‘a): university
  6. الْكِتَاب (al-kitaab): book
  7. الْقَلَم (al-qalam): pen
  8. الدَّفْتَر (ad-daftar): notebook
  9. الصَّفّ (as-saff): class, classroom
  10. تَعَلَّمَ (ta‘allama): to learn
