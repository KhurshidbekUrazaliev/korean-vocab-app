Day 35: Bank and post الْبَنْك وَالْبَرِيد

Learn 10 Arabic words about bank and post: الْبَنْك (bank), الْحِسَاب (account), الْعُمْلَة (currency), الصَّرَّافُ الْآلِيّ (cash machine) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْبَنْك (al-bank): bank
  2. الْحِسَاب (al-hisaab): account
  3. الْعُمْلَة (al-‘umla): currency
  4. الصَّرَّافُ الْآلِيّ (as-sarraaf al-aalii): cash machine, ATM
  5. الْقَرْض (al-qard): loan
  6. سَحَبَ (sahaba): to withdraw
  7. الْبَرِيد (al-bariid): post, mail
  8. الْمُغَلَّف (al-mughallaf): envelope
  9. الْعُنْوَان (al-‘unwaan): address
  10. أَرْسَلَ (arsala): to send
