Day 43: Beach and camping الشَّاطِئ وَالتَّخْيِيم

Learn 10 Arabic words about beach and camping: الشَّاطِئ (beach), الْبَحْر (sea), الرَّمْل (sand), الْأَمْوَاج (waves) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الشَّاطِئ (ash-shaati’): beach
  2. الْبَحْر (al-bahr): sea
  3. الرَّمْل (ar-raml): sand
  4. الْأَمْوَاج (al-amwaaj): waves
  5. السِّبَاحَة (as-sibaaha): swimming
  6. سَبَحَ (sabaha): to swim
  7. الْخَيْمَة (al-khayma): tent
  8. الْمُخَيَّم (al-mukhayyam): campsite
  9. الشِّوَاء (ash-shiwaa’): barbecue
  10. وَاقِي الشَّمْس (waaqii ash-shams): sunscreen
