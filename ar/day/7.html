Day 07: Time of day أَوْقَاتُ الْيَوْم

Learn 10 Arabic words about time of day: الصَّبَاح (morning), بَعْدَ الظُّهْر (afternoon), الْمَسَاء (evening), اللَّيْل (night) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الصَّبَاح (as-sabaah): morning
  2. بَعْدَ الظُّهْر (ba‘da adh-dhuhr): afternoon
  3. الْمَسَاء (al-masaa’): evening
  4. اللَّيْل (al-layl): night
  5. الْيَوْم (al-yawm): today, day
  6. غَدًا (ghadan): tomorrow
  7. أَمْس (ams): yesterday
  8. الْآن (al-aan): now
  9. لَاحِقًا (laahiqan): later
  10. قَرِيبًا (qariiban): soon
