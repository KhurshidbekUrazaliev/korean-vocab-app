Day 26: Meals الْوَجَبَات

Learn 10 Arabic words about meals: الْفُطُور (breakfast), الْغَدَاء (lunch), الْعَشَاء (dinner), الشُّورْبَة (soup) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْفُطُور (al-futuur): breakfast
  2. الْغَدَاء (al-ghadaa’): lunch
  3. الْعَشَاء (al-‘ashaa’): dinner
  4. الشُّورْبَة (ash-shuurba): soup
  5. السَّلَطَة (as-salata): salad
  6. الْحَلْوَى (al-halwaa): dessert, sweets
  7. الْبَطَاطَا الْمَقْلِيَّة (al-bataataa al-maqliyya): chips, fries
  8. الْفَلَافِل (al-falaafil): falafel
  9. اللَّحْمُ الْمَشْوِيّ (al-lahm al-mashwii): grilled meat
  10. الْمُثَلَّجَات (al-muthallajaat): ice cream
