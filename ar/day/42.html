Day 42: Sightseeing السِّيَاحَة

Learn 10 Arabic words about sightseeing: السَّائِح (tourist), الْقَصْر (palace), التِّمْثَال (statue), الْكَامِيرَا (camera) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. السَّائِح (as-saa’ih): tourist
  2. الْقَصْر (al-qasr): palace, castle
  3. التِّمْثَال (at-timthaal): statue, monument
  4. الْكَامِيرَا (al-kaamiiraa): camera
  5. الْجَوْلَةُ السِّيَاحِيَّة (al-jawla as-siyaahiyya): guided tour
  6. حَجَزَ (hajaza): to book
  7. زَارَ (zaara): to visit
  8. الِاسْتِقْبَال (al-istiqbaal): reception
  9. غُرْفَةٌ لِشَخْصَيْن (ghurfatun li-shakhsayn): double room
  10. الْمَسْرَح (al-masrah): theatre
