Day 13: On the road عَلَى الطَّرِيق

Learn 10 Arabic words about on the road: الشَّارِع (street), الطَّرِيق (road), الْجِسْر (bridge), السَّائِق (driver) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الشَّارِع (ash-shaari‘): street
  2. الطَّرِيق (at-tariiq): road, route
  3. الْجِسْر (al-jisr): bridge
  4. السَّائِق (as-saa’iq): driver
  5. الرَّاكِب (ar-raakib): passenger
  6. زَحْمَةُ السَّيْر (zahmat as-sayr): traffic jam
  7. مَحَطَّةُ الْوَقُود (mahattat al-waquud): petrol station
  8. الْمَوْقِف (al-mawqif): parking space
  9. الْحَادِث (al-haadith): accident
  10. حِزَامُ الْأَمَان (hizaam al-amaan): seatbelt
