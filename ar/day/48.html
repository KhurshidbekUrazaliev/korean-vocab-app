Day 48: Nature الطَّبِيعَة

Learn 10 Arabic words about nature: الْجَبَل (mountain), النَّهْر (river), الصَّحْرَاء (desert), الْغَابَة (forest) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْجَبَل (al-jabal): mountain
  2. النَّهْر (an-nahr): river
  3. الصَّحْرَاء (as-sahraa’): desert
  4. الْغَابَة (al-ghaaba): forest
  5. الْجَزِيرَة (al-jaziira): island
  6. الْوَادِي (al-waadii): valley
  7. الْبُحَيْرَة (al-buhayra): lake
  8. الشَّمْس (ash-shams): sun
  9. الْقَمَر (al-qamar): moon
  10. النُّجُوم (an-nujuum): stars
