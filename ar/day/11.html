Day 11: Around town فِي الْمَدِينَة

Learn 10 Arabic words about around town: الْمَسْجِد (mosque), الْمُسْتَشْفَى (hospital), الْفُنْدُق (hotel), الْمَطْعَم (restaurant) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمَسْجِد (al-masjid): mosque
  2. الْمُسْتَشْفَى (al-mustashfaa): hospital
  3. الْفُنْدُق (al-funduq): hotel
  4. الْمَطْعَم (al-mat‘am): restaurant
  5. الْمَدْرَسَة (al-madrasa): school
  6. الْمَتْجَر (al-matjar): shop
  7. السُّوق (as-suuq): market
  8. الْمَكْتَبَة (al-maktaba): library, bookshop
  9. الْمَتْحَف (al-mathaf): museum
  10. الْمَقْهَى (al-maqhaa): café
