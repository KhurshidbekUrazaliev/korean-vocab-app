Day 38: At the doctor's عِنْدَ الطَّبِيب

Learn 10 Arabic words about at the doctor's: الْمَرِيض (patient), الدَّوَاء (medicine), الْأَلَم (pain), الْمَوْعِد (appointment) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمَرِيض (al-mariid): patient
  2. الدَّوَاء (ad-dawaa’): medicine
  3. الْأَلَم (al-alam): pain
  4. الْمَوْعِد (al-maw‘id): appointment
  5. الْوَصْفَةُ الطِّبِّيَّة (al-wasfa at-tibbiyya): prescription
  6. الْحُمَّى (al-hummaa): fever
  7. الزُّكَام (az-zukaam): cold
  8. الصُّدَاع (as-sudaa‘): headache
  9. السُّعَال (as-su‘aal): cough
  10. الْعِيَادَة (al-‘iyaada): clinic
