Day 15: At the airport فِي الْمَطَار

Learn 10 Arabic words about at the airport: الْمَطَار (airport), جَوَازُ السَّفَر (passport), الْجَمَارِك (customs), الرِّحْلَة (flight) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمَطَار (al-mataar): airport
  2. جَوَازُ السَّفَر (jawaaz as-safar): passport
  3. الْجَمَارِك (al-jamaarik): customs
  4. الرِّحْلَة (ar-rihla): flight, trip
  5. بِطَاقَةُ الصُّعُود (bitaaqat as-su‘uud): boarding pass
  6. حَقِيبَةُ السَّفَر (haqiibat as-safar): suitcase
  7. الطَّيَّار (at-tayyaar): pilot
  8. الْمُغَادَرَة (al-mughaadara): departures
  9. الْوُصُول (al-wusuul): arrivals
  10. الْأَمْتِعَة (al-amti‘a): luggage
