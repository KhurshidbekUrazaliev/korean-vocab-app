Day 02: Numbers الْأَعْدَاد

Learn 10 Arabic words about numbers: وَاحِد (one), اِثْنَان (two), ثَلَاثَة (three), أَرْبَعَة (four) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. وَاحِد (waahid): one
  2. اِثْنَان (ithnaan): two
  3. ثَلَاثَة (thalaatha): three
  4. أَرْبَعَة (arba‘a): four
  5. خَمْسَة (khamsa): five
  6. سِتَّة (sitta): six
  7. سَبْعَة (sab‘a): seven
  8. ثَمَانِيَة (thamaaniya): eight
  9. تِسْعَة (tis‘a): nine
  10. عَشَرَة (‘ashara): ten
