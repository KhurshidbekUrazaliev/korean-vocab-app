Day 34: Phone and internet الْهَاتِف وَالْإِنْتَرْنِت

Learn 10 Arabic words about phone and internet: الْهَاتِف (telephone), الْجَوَّال (mobile phone), الْإِنْتَرْنِت (internet), الْبَرِيدُ الْإِلِكْتُرُونِيّ (email) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْهَاتِف (al-haatif): telephone
  2. الْجَوَّال (al-jawwaal): mobile phone
  3. الْإِنْتَرْنِت (al-intarnit): internet
  4. الْبَرِيدُ الْإِلِكْتُرُونِيّ (al-bariid al-ilikturuunii): email
  5. الرِّسَالَة (ar-risaala): message, letter
  6. كَلِمَةُ السِّرّ (kalimat as-sirr): password
  7. الشَّاشَة (ash-shaasha): screen
  8. الْمَوْقِع (al-mawqi‘): website, site
  9. الْبَطَّارِيَّة (al-battaariyya): battery
  10. اِتَّصَلَ (ittasala): to call
