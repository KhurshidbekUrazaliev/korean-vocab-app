Day 09: Weather and seasons الطَّقْس وَالْفُصُول

Learn 10 Arabic words about weather and seasons: الصَّيْف (summer), الْخَرِيف (autumn), الشِّتَاء (winter), الرَّبِيع (spring) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الصَّيْف (as-sayf): summer
  2. الْخَرِيف (al-khariif): autumn
  3. الشِّتَاء (ash-shitaa’): winter
  4. الرَّبِيع (ar-rabii‘): spring
  5. الْمَطَر (al-matar): rain
  6. الثَّلْج (ath-thalj): snow
  7. حَارّ (haarr): hot
  8. دَافِئ (daafi’): warm
  9. رَطْب (ratb): humid
  10. الْعَاصِفَة (al-‘aasifa): storm, gale
