Day 46: Pets and farm animals الْحَيَوَانَاتُ الْأَلِيفَة

Learn 10 Arabic words about pets and farm animals: الْقِطّ (cat), الْكَلْب (dog), الْحِصَان (horse), الْجَمَل (camel) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْقِطّ (al-qitt): cat
  2. الْكَلْب (al-kalb): dog
  3. الْحِصَان (al-hisaan): horse
  4. الْجَمَل (al-jamal): camel
  5. الْبَقَرَة (al-baqara): cow
  6. الْخَرُوف (al-kharuuf): sheep
  7. الدَّجَاجَة (ad-dajaaja): hen
  8. الْمَاعِز (al-maa‘iz): goat
  9. الْحِمَار (al-himaar): donkey
  10. الطَّائِر (at-taa’ir): bird
