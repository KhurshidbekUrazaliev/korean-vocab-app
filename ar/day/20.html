Day 20: Bedroom and bathroom غُرْفَةُ النَّوْم وَالْحَمَّام

Learn 10 Arabic words about bedroom and bathroom: السَّرِير (bed), الْبَطَّانِيَّة (blanket), خِزَانَةُ الْمَلَابِس (wardrobe), الْحَمَّام (bathroom) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. السَّرِير (as-sariir): bed
  2. الْبَطَّانِيَّة (al-battaaniyya): blanket
  3. خِزَانَةُ الْمَلَابِس (khizaanat al-malaabis): wardrobe
  4. الْحَمَّام (al-hammaam): bathroom, toilet
  5. الصَّابُون (as-saabuun): soap
  6. الْمِنْشَفَة (al-minshafa): towel
  7. فُرْشَاةُ الْأَسْنَان (furshaat al-asnaan): toothbrush
  8. مَعْجُونُ الْأَسْنَان (ma‘juun al-asnaan): toothpaste
  9. الْمُشْط (al-musht): comb
  10. نَامَ (naama): to sleep
