Day 05: Feelings الْمَشَاعِر

Learn 10 Arabic words about feelings: سَعِيد (happy), حَزِين (sad), غَضْبَان (angry), قَلِق (worried) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. سَعِيد (sa‘iid): happy
  2. حَزِين (haziin): sad
  3. غَضْبَان (ghadbaan): angry
  4. قَلِق (qaliq): worried
  5. خَائِف (khaa’if): afraid
  6. مُتَحَمِّس (mutahammis): excited
  7. جَيِّد (jayyid): good, well
  8. مَرِيض (mariid): ill, unwell
  9. أَفْضَل (afdal): better
  10. أَسْوَأ (aswa’): worse
