Day 19: The kitchen الْمَطْبَخ

Learn 10 Arabic words about the kitchen: الْمَطْبَخ (kitchen), الثَّلَّاجَة (fridge), الْفُرْن (oven), السِّكِّين (knife) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمَطْبَخ (al-matbakh): kitchen
  2. الثَّلَّاجَة (ath-thallaaja): fridge
  3. الْفُرْن (al-furn): oven
  4. السِّكِّين (as-sikkiin): knife
  5. الشَّوْكَة (ash-shawka): fork
  6. الْمِلْعَقَة (al-mil‘aqa): spoon
  7. الصَّحْن (as-sahn): plate
  8. الْكُوب (al-kuub): cup, glass
  9. الْقِدْر (al-qidr): saucepan, pot
  10. الْمِقْلَاة (al-miqlaa): frying pan
