Day 28: Clothes الْمَلَابِس

Learn 10 Arabic words about clothes: الْقَمِيص (shirt), الْفُسْتَان (dress), الْبَنْطَال (trousers), التَّنُّورَة (skirt) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْقَمِيص (al-qamiis): shirt
  2. الْفُسْتَان (al-fustaan): dress
  3. الْبَنْطَال (al-bantaal): trousers
  4. التَّنُّورَة (at-tannuura): skirt
  5. الْحِذَاء (al-hidhaa’): shoe
  6. الْمِعْطَف (al-mi‘taf): coat
  7. السُّتْرَة (as-sutra): jacket
  8. الْقُبَّعَة (al-qubba‘a): hat
  9. الْحِزَام (al-hizaam): belt
  10. الشَّال (ash-shaal): scarf
