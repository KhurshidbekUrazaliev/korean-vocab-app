Day 04: Relatives and friends الْأَقَارِب وَالْأَصْدِقَاء

Learn 10 Arabic words about relatives and friends: الْعَمّ (paternal uncle), الْعَمَّة (paternal aunt), الْحَفِيد (grandson), الْحَفِيدَة (granddaughter) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْعَمّ (al-‘amm): paternal uncle
  2. الْعَمَّة (al-‘amma): paternal aunt
  3. الْحَفِيد (al-hafiid): grandson
  4. الْحَفِيدَة (al-hafiida): granddaughter
  5. الطِّفْل (at-tifl): child, baby
  6. الْمُرَاهِق (al-muraahiq): teenager
  7. الْأَهْل (al-ahl): parents, family
  8. اِبْنُ الْعَمّ (ibn al-‘amm): male cousin (paternal uncle's son)
  9. الصَّدِيق (as-sadiiq): friend
  10. الزَّمِيل (az-zamiil): colleague
