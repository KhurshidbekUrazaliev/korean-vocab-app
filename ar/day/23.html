Day 23: Fruit الْفَوَاكِه

Learn 10 Arabic words about fruit: التُّفَّاح (apple), الْمَوْز (banana), الْبُرْتُقَال (orange), الْعِنَب (grapes) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. التُّفَّاح (at-tuffaah): apple
  2. الْمَوْز (al-mawz): banana
  3. الْبُرْتُقَال (al-burtuqaal): orange
  4. الْعِنَب (al-‘inab): grapes
  5. اللَّيْمُون (al-laymuun): lemon
  6. التَّمْر (at-tamr): dates
  7. التِّين (at-tiin): figs
  8. الرُّمَّان (ar-rummaan): pomegranate
  9. الْبِطِّيخ (al-bittiikh): watermelon
  10. الْفَرَاوْلَة (al-faraawla): strawberries
