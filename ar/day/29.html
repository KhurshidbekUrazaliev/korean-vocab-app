Day 29: Bags and jewellery الْحَقَائِب وَالْمُجَوْهَرَات

Learn 10 Arabic words about bags and jewellery: حَقِيبَةُ الْيَد (handbag), الْمِحْفَظَة (wallet), الْمِظَلَّة (umbrella), نَظَّارَةُ الشَّمْس (sunglasses) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. حَقِيبَةُ الْيَد (haqiibat al-yad): handbag
  2. الْمِحْفَظَة (al-mihfadha): wallet
  3. الْمِظَلَّة (al-midhalla): umbrella
  4. نَظَّارَةُ الشَّمْس (nadhdhaarat ash-shams): sunglasses
  5. الْقِلَادَة (al-qilaada): necklace
  6. الْإِسْوَارَة (al-iswaara): bracelet
  7. الْخَاتَم (al-khaatam): ring
  8. الْقُفَّازَات (al-quffaazaat): gloves
  9. الْقُطْن (al-qutn): cotton
  10. الصُّوف (as-suuf): wool
