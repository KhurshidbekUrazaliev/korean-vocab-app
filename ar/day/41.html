Day 41: Free time وَقْتُ الْفَرَاغ

Learn 10 Arabic words about free time: الْهِوَايَة (hobby), الْقِرَاءَة (reading), السَّفَر (travelling), الْمَشْي (walking) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْهِوَايَة (al-hiwaaya): hobby
  2. الْقِرَاءَة (al-qiraa’a): reading
  3. السَّفَر (as-safar): travelling
  4. الْمَشْي (al-mashy): walking
  5. الطَّبْخ (at-tabkh): cooking
  6. التَّسَوُّق (at-tasawwuq): shopping
  7. الْمُوسِيقَى (al-muusiiqaa): music
  8. الْفِيلْم (al-fiilm): film
  9. السِّينَمَا (as-siinamaa): cinema
  10. الْإِجَازَة (al-ijaaza): holiday
