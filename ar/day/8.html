Day 08: Days of the week أَيَّامُ الْأُسْبُوع

Learn 10 Arabic words about days of the week: الْأَحَد (Sunday), الِاثْنَيْن (Monday), الثُّلَاثَاء (Tuesday), الْأَرْبِعَاء (Wednesday) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْأَحَد (al-ahad): Sunday
  2. الِاثْنَيْن (al-ithnayn): Monday
  3. الثُّلَاثَاء (ath-thulaathaa’): Tuesday
  4. الْأَرْبِعَاء (al-arbi‘aa’): Wednesday
  5. الْخَمِيس (al-khamiis): Thursday
  6. الْجُمُعَة (al-jumu‘a): Friday
  7. السَّبْت (as-sabt): Saturday
  8. الْأُسْبُوع (al-usbuu‘): week
  9. الشَّهْر (ash-shahr): month
  10. السَّنَة (as-sana): year
