Day 33: At work فِي الْعَمَل

Learn 10 Arabic words about at work: الْمُدِير (manager), الشَّرِكَة (company), الِاجْتِمَاع (meeting), الْمَكْتَب (office) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمُدِير (al-mudiir): manager
  2. الشَّرِكَة (ash-sharika): company
  3. الِاجْتِمَاع (al-ijtimaa‘): meeting
  4. الْمَكْتَب (al-maktab): office, desk
  5. التَّقْرِير (at-taqriir): report
  6. الزَّبُون (az-zabuun): client, customer
  7. الْعَرْض (al-‘ard): presentation
  8. الْحَاسُوب (al-haasuub): computer
  9. الطَّابِعَة (at-taabi‘a): printer
  10. فَرِيقُ الْعَمَل (fariiq al-‘amal): staff, team
