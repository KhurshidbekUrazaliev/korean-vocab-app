Day 36: The body الْجِسْم

Learn 10 Arabic words about the body: الرَّأْس (head), الْوَجْه (face), الْعَيْن (eye), الْأُذُن (ear) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الرَّأْس (ar-ra’s): head
  2. الْوَجْه (al-wajh): face
  3. الْعَيْن (al-‘ayn): eye
  4. الْأُذُن (al-udhun): ear
  5. الْأَنْف (al-anf): nose
  6. الْفَم (al-fam): mouth
  7. الشَّعْر (ash-sha‘r): hair
  8. الْيَد (al-yad): hand
  9. الرِّجْل (ar-rijl): leg
  10. الْقَلْب (al-qalb): heart
