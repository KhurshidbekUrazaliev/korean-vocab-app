Day 14: Trains and buses الْقِطَارَات وَالْحَافِلَات

Learn 10 Arabic words about trains and buses: الْمَحَطَّة (station), التَّذْكِرَة (ticket), الرَّصِيف (platform), مَوْقِفُ الْحَافِلَة (bus stop) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْمَحَطَّة (al-mahatta): station
  2. التَّذْكِرَة (at-tadhkira): ticket
  3. الرَّصِيف (ar-rasiif): platform
  4. مَوْقِفُ الْحَافِلَة (mawqif al-haafila): bus stop
  5. الْأُجْرَة (al-ujra): fare
  6. الْجَدْوَل (al-jadwal): timetable
  7. مَكْتَبُ التَّذَاكِر (maktab at-tadhaakir): ticket office
  8. الدَّرَجَةُ الْأُولَى (ad-daraja al-uulaa): first class
  9. الْمَقْعَد (al-maq‘ad): seat
  10. الْخَطّ (al-khatt): line
