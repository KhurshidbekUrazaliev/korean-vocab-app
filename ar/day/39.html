Day 39: Emergencies الطَّوَارِئ

Learn 10 Arabic words about emergencies: الْإِسْعَاف (ambulance), الْإِسْعَافَاتُ الْأَوَّلِيَّة (first aid), الْجُرْح (cut), الْكَسْر (fracture) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. الْإِسْعَاف (al-is‘aaf): ambulance
  2. الْإِسْعَافَاتُ الْأَوَّلِيَّة (al-is‘aafaat al-awwaliyya): first aid
  3. الْجُرْح (al-jurh): cut, wound
  4. الْكَسْر (al-kasr): fracture
  5. الْحَرْق (al-harq): burn
  6. النَّجْدَة (an-najda): help!
  7. الشُّرْطَة (ash-shurta): police
  8. رَجُلُ الْإِطْفَاء (rajul al-itfaa’): firefighter
  9. الْحَرِيق (al-hariiq): fire
  10. وَقَعَ (waqa‘a): to fall
