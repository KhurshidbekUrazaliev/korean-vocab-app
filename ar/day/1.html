Day 01: Greetings التَّحِيَّات

Learn 10 Arabic words about greetings: مَرْحَبًا (hello), صَبَاحُ الْخَيْر (good morning), مَسَاءُ الْخَيْر (good evening), مَعَ السَّلَامَة (goodbye) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. مَرْحَبًا (marhaban): hello
  2. صَبَاحُ الْخَيْر (sabaah al-khayr): good morning
  3. مَسَاءُ الْخَيْر (masaa’ al-khayr): good evening
  4. مَعَ السَّلَامَة (ma‘a as-salaama): goodbye
  5. شُكْرًا (shukran): thank you
  6. عَفْوًا (‘afwan): you're welcome, pardon
  7. نَعَم (na‘am): yes
  8. لَا (laa): no
  9. رَجَاءً (rajaa’an): please
  10. آسِف (aasif): sorry
