Day 21: Shopping التَّسَوُّق

Learn 10 Arabic words about shopping: اِشْتَرَى (to buy), دَفَعَ (to pay), السِّعْر (price), النُّقُود (money) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. اِشْتَرَى (ishtaraa): to buy
  2. دَفَعَ (dafa‘a): to pay
  3. السِّعْر (as-si‘r): price
  4. النُّقُود (an-nuquud): money, cash
  5. الْبِطَاقَةُ الِائْتِمَانِيَّة (al-bitaaqa al-i’timaaniyya): credit card
  6. الْإِيصَال (al-iisaal): receipt
  7. الْكِيس (al-kiis): bag
  8. الْفَكَّة (al-fakka): change, small coins
  9. السَّلَّة (as-salla): basket
  10. الْمَرْكَزُ التِّجَارِيّ (al-markaz at-tijaarii): shopping centre, mall
