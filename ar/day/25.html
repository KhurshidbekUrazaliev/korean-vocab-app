Day 25: Meat, fish and dairy اللُّحُوم وَالْأَسْمَاك وَالْأَلْبَان

Learn 10 Arabic words about meat, fish and dairy: اللَّحْم (meat), الدَّجَاج (chicken), السَّمَك (fish), الْبَيْض (eggs) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. اللَّحْم (al-lahm): meat
  2. الدَّجَاج (ad-dajaaj): chicken
  3. السَّمَك (as-samak): fish
  4. الْبَيْض (al-bayd): eggs
  5. الْجُبْن (al-jubn): cheese
  6. الزُّبْدَة (az-zubda): butter
  7. الزَّبَادِي (az-zabaadii): yoghurt
  8. لَحْمُ الْبَقَر (lahm al-baqar): beef
  9. لَحْمُ الْغَنَم (lahm al-ghanam): lamb
  10. الرُّوبْيَان (ar-ruubyaan): prawns, shrimp
