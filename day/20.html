Day 20: At the post office 우체국에서

Learn 10 Korean words about at the post office: 우체국 (post office), 우표 (postage stamp), 봉투 (envelope), 편지 (letter) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 우체국 (ucheguk): post office
  2. 우표 (upyo): postage stamp
  3. 봉투 (bongtu): envelope
  4. 편지 (pyeonji): letter
  5. 소포 (sopo): parcel
  6. 주소 (juso): address
  7. 우편 번호 (upyeon beonho): postal code
  8. 우체통 (uchetong): mailbox for outgoing mail
  9. 보내다 (bonaeda): to send
  10. 받는 사람 (banneun saram): recipient
