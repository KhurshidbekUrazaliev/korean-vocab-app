Day 02: Travel Prep 여행 준비

Learn 10 Korean words about travel prep: 비자 (visa), 발급 (issuance), 해외여행 (overseas travel), 일정 (schedule) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 비자 (bija): visa
  2. 발급 (balgeup): issuance, to issue
  3. 해외여행 (haeoeyeohaeng): overseas travel
  4. 일정 (iljeong): schedule, itinerary
  5. 일행 (ilhaeng): travel companion, party
  6. 전통 (jeontong): tradition
  7. 체험 (cheheom): experience
  8. 제안하다 (jeanhada): to suggest, to propose
  9. 출국하다 (chulgukhada): to leave the country
  10. 싸다 (ssada): to pack, to wrap
