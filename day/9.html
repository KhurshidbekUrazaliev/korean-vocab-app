Day 09: Daily Life 일상생활

Learn 10 Korean words about daily life: 늦잠 (oversleeping), 대표 (representative), 발표 (presentation), 평가 (evaluation) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 늦잠 (neutjam): oversleeping, sleeping in
  2. 대표 (daepyo): representative, president
  3. 발표 (balpyo): presentation, announcement
  4. 평가 (pyeongga): evaluation, assessment
  5. 면접 (myeonjeop): interview
  6. 지각하다 (jigakhada): to be late, to perceive
  7. 인생 (insaeng): life
  8. 방해하다 (banghaehada): to disturb, to interfere
  9. 버릇 (beoreut): habit, manners
  10. 해결하다 (haegyeolhada): to solve, to resolve
