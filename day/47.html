Day 47: Cycling by the river 강변에서 자전거 타기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about cycling by the river: 자전거 (bicycle), 강변 (riverside), 페달 (pedal), 체인 (chain) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 자전거 (jajeongeo): bicycle
  2. 강변 (gangbyeon): riverside
  3. 페달 (pedal): pedal
  4. 체인 (chein): chain
  5. 안장 (anjang): saddle
  6. 브레이크 (beureikeu): brake
  7. 헬멧 (helmet): helmet
  8. 자전거 도로 (jajeongeo doro): bicycle path
  9. 바퀴 (bakwi): wheel
  10. 강바람 (gangbaram): river breeze
