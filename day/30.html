Day 30: Hiking in the mountains 산에서 등산하기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about hiking in the mountains: 등산 (mountain hiking), 등산화 (hiking boots), 등산로 (hiking trail), 정상 (summit) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 등산 (deungsan): mountain hiking
  2. 등산화 (deungsanhwa): hiking boots
  3. 등산로 (deungsanno): hiking trail
  4. 정상 (jeongsang): summit
  5. 계곡 (gyegok): mountain valley
  6. 능선 (neungseon): ridgeline
  7. 바위 (bawi): boulder
  8. 오르막길 (oreumakgil): uphill path
  9. 산기슭 (sangiseuk): foot of a mountain
  10. 하산하다 (hasanhada): to descend a mountain
