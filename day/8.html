Day 08: Culture & Freedom 문화와 자유

Learn 10 Korean words about culture & freedom: 서로 (each other), 별명 (nickname), 반말 (informal speech), 마음대로 (as one pleases) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 서로 (seoro): each other, mutually
  2. 별명 (byeolmyeong): nickname
  3. 반말 (banmal): informal speech
  4. 마음대로 (maeumdaero): as one pleases, freely
  5. 반팔 (banpal): short sleeves
  6. 슬리퍼 (seullipeo): slippers
  7. 자유롭다 (jayuropda): to be free
  8. 굉장히 (goengjanghi): extremely, very
  9. 낯설다 (natseolda): to be unfamiliar, strange
  10. 익숙하다 (iksukhada): to be familiar, accustomed
