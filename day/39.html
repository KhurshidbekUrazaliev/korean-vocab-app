Day 39: A birthday celebration 생일 축하

Learn 10 Korean words about a birthday celebration: 생일 (birthday), 생신 (birthday (honorific)), 미역국 (seaweed soup), 케이크 (cake) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 생일 (saengil): birthday
  2. 생신 (saengsin): birthday (honorific)
  3. 미역국 (miyeokguk): seaweed soup
  4. 케이크 (keikeu): cake
  5. 촛불 (chotbul): candle flame
  6. 선물 (seonmul): gift
  7. 소원 (sowon): wish
  8. 초대하다 (chodaehada): to invite
  9. 축하하다 (chukahada): to congratulate or celebrate
  10. 생일상 (saengilsang): meal laid out for a birthday
