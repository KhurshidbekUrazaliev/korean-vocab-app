Day 25: At the bakery 빵집에서

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about at the bakery: 빵집 (bakery), 식빵 (sandwich loaf), 단팥빵 (sweet red bean bun), 바게트 (baguette) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 빵집 (ppangjip): bakery
  2. 식빵 (sikppang): sandwich loaf
  3. 단팥빵 (danpatppang): sweet red bean bun
  4. 바게트 (bageteu): baguette
  5. 밀가루 (milgaru): wheat flour
  6. 버터 (beoteo): butter
  7. 반죽 (banjuk): dough
  8. 오븐 (obeun): oven
  9. 집게 (jipge): tongs
  10. 쟁반 (jaengban): serving tray
