Day 07: Travel 여행

Learn 10 Korean words about travel: 기대하다 (to expect), 좌석 (seat), 앞뒤 (front and back), 공간 (space) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 기대하다 (gidaehada): to expect, to look forward to
  2. 좌석 (jwaseok): seat
  3. 앞뒤 (apdwi): front and back
  4. 공간 (gonggan): space
  5. 펴다 (pyeoda): to spread, to unfold
  6. 창밖 (changbak): outside the window
  7. 풍경 (punggyeong): scenery, landscape
  8. 어지럽다 (eojireopda): to be dizzy, messy
  9. 등 (deung): back (body part)
  10. 건조하다 (geonjohada): to be dry
