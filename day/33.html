Day 33: At the library 도서관에서

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about at the library: 도서관 (library), 사서 (librarian), 서가 (bookshelf), 열람실 (reading room) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 도서관 (doseogwan): library
  2. 사서 (saseo): librarian
  3. 서가 (seoga): bookshelf
  4. 열람실 (yeollamsil): reading room
  5. 소설 (soseol): novel
  6. 사전 (sajeon): dictionary
  7. 대출하다 (daechulhada): to check out a book
  8. 반납하다 (bannapada): to return a borrowed item
  9. 연체 (yeonche): overdue status
  10. 회원증 (hoewonjeung): membership card
