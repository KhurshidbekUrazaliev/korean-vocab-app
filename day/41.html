Day 41: At the hair salon 미용실에서

Learn 10 Korean words about at the hair salon: 미용실 (hair salon), 미용사 (hairstylist), 앞머리 (bangs (fringe)), 염색 (hair coloring) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 미용실 (miyongsil): hair salon
  2. 미용사 (miyongsa): hairstylist
  3. 앞머리 (ammeori): bangs (fringe)
  4. 염색 (yeomsaek): hair coloring
  5. 파마 (pama): perm
  6. 단발머리 (danbalmeori): bobbed hair
  7. 곱슬머리 (gopseulmeori): curly hair
  8. 빗 (bit): comb
  9. 자르다 (jareuda): to cut
  10. 다듬다 (dadeumda): to trim or tidy up
