Day 06: Family & Home 가족과 가정

Learn 10 Korean words about family & home: 일기 (diary), 외롭다 (to be lonely), 사실 (fact), 가정 (home) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 일기 (ilgi): diary
  2. 외롭다 (oeropta): to be lonely
  3. 사실 (sasil): fact, truth
  4. 가정 (gajeong): home, household
  5. 부모 (bumo): parents
  6. 역할 (yeokhal): role
  7. 직장 (jikjang): workplace, job
  8. 키우다 (kiuda): to raise, to grow
  9. 추억 (chueok): memory
  10. 노력하다 (noryeokhada): to make an effort
