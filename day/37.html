Day 37: A pottery class 도예 수업

Learn 10 Korean words about a pottery class: 도예 (ceramic art), 점토 (clay), 물레 (potter's wheel), 가마 (kiln) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 도예 (doye): ceramic art
  2. 점토 (jeomto): clay
  3. 물레 (mulle): potter's wheel
  4. 가마 (gama): kiln
  5. 유약 (yuyak): glaze for pottery
  6. 항아리 (hangari): earthenware jar
  7. 꽃병 (kkotbyeong): flower vase
  8. 그릇 (geureut): bowl or dish
  9. 빚다 (bitda): to shape by hand
  10. 굽다 (gupda): to fire pottery
