Day 43: Recycling at home 집에서 재활용하기

Learn 10 Korean words about recycling at home: 재활용 (recycling), 분리배출 (separating waste for collection), 폐지 (waste paper), 유리병 (glass bottle) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 재활용 (jaehwaryong): recycling
  2. 분리배출 (bullibaechul): separating waste for collection
  3. 폐지 (pyeji): waste paper
  4. 유리병 (yuribyeong): glass bottle
  5. 플라스틱 (peullaseutik): plastic
  6. 캔 (kaen): metal can
  7. 헌 옷 (heon ot): old clothes, worn clothing
  8. 뚜껑 (ttukkeong): lid
  9. 비우다 (biuda): to empty
  10. 라벨 (rabel): label
