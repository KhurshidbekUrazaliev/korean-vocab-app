Day 16: Riding the subway 지하철 타기

Learn 10 Korean words about riding the subway: 지하철 (subway), 노선도 (route map), 환승 (transfer between lines), 승강장 (platform) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 지하철 (jihacheol): subway
  2. 노선도 (noseondo): route map
  3. 환승 (hwanseung): transfer between lines
  4. 승강장 (seunggangjang): platform
  5. 개찰구 (gaechalgu): ticket gate
  6. 교통 카드 (gyotong kadeu): transit card
  7. 손잡이 (sonjabi): handgrip
  8. 첫차 (cheotcha): first train, first bus
  9. 막차 (makcha): last train, last bus
  10. 내리다 (naerida): to get off
