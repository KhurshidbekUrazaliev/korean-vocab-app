Day 49: Household repairs 집수리

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about household repairs: 집수리 (home repairs), 공구함 (toolbox), 망치 (hammer), 드라이버 (screwdriver) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 집수리 (jipsuri): home repairs
  2. 공구함 (gongguham): toolbox
  3. 망치 (mangchi): hammer
  4. 드라이버 (deuraibeo): screwdriver
  5. 나사 (nasa): screw
  6. 못 (mot): nail
  7. 경첩 (gyeongcheop): hinge
  8. 문고리 (mungori): door handle
  9. 조이다 (joida): to tighten
  10. 고치다 (gochida): to repair
