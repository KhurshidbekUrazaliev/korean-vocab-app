Day 28: At the pharmacy 약국에서

Learn 10 Korean words about at the pharmacy: 약국 (pharmacy), 약사 (pharmacist), 알약 (pill), 연고 (ointment) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 약국 (yakguk): pharmacy
  2. 약사 (yaksa): pharmacist
  3. 알약 (allyak): pill
  4. 연고 (yeongo): ointment
  5. 진통제 (jintongje): pain reliever
  6. 소화제 (sohwaje): digestive medicine
  7. 기침약 (gichimnyak): cough medicine
  8. 복용하다 (bogyonghada): to take medicine
  9. 식전 (sikjeon): before a meal
  10. 식후 (sikhu): after a meal
