Day 14: Work & Time 일과 시간

Learn 10 Korean words about work & time: 종일 (all day long), 쟤 (that person (casual)), 보고서 (report), 제출하다 (to submit) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 종일 (jongil): all day long
  2. 쟤 (jyae): that person (casual)
  3. 보고서 (bogoseo): report
  4. 제출하다 (jechulhada): to submit, hand in
  5. 전날 (jeonnal): the day before, previous day
  6. 아끼다 (akkida): to treasure, save, spare
  7. 겨우 (gyeou): barely, only, just
  8. 마치다 (machida): to finish, complete
  9. 회식 (hoesik): company dinner, work gathering
  10. 기억나다 (gieongnada): to remember, recall
