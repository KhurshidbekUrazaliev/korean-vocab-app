Day 13: Gardening & Weather 식물과 날씨

Learn 10 Korean words about gardening & weather: 심다 (to plant), 베란다 (veranda), 화분 (flower pot), 가꾸다 (to cultivate) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 심다 (simda): to plant
  2. 베란다 (beranda): veranda, balcony
  3. 화분 (hwabun): flower pot
  4. 가꾸다 (gakkuda): to cultivate, grow, tend to
  5. 피다 (pida): to bloom, flower
  6. 장마 (jangma): rainy season, monsoon
  7. 오랫동안 (oraettongan): for a long time
  8. 야외 (yaoe): outdoors, open air
  9. 실내 (sillae): indoors
  10. 막다 (makda): to block, stop, prevent
