Day 11: Personality 성격

Learn 10 Korean words about personality: 성격 (personality), 활발하다 (to be active), 부럽다 (to be envious), 부족하다 (to be insufficient) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 성격 (seonggyeok): personality
  2. 활발하다 (hwalbalhada): to be active, lively
  3. 부럽다 (bureopda): to be envious
  4. 부족하다 (bujokada): to be insufficient
  5. 겸손하다 (gyeomsonhada): to be humble
  6. 장점 (jangjeom): strength, advantage
  7. 물론 (mullon): of course
  8. 단점 (danjeom): weakness, disadvantage
  9. 거절 (geojeol): refusal, rejection
  10. 들어주다 (deureojuda): to listen, grant a request
