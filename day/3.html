Day 03: Student Life 학교생활

Learn 10 Korean words about student life: 유학생 (international student), 동아리 활동 (club activities), 방법 (method), 지원 (support) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 유학생 (yuhaksaeng): international student
  2. 동아리 활동 (dongari hwaldong): club activities
  3. 방법 (bangbeop): method, way
  4. 지원 (jiwon): support, aid
  5. 센터 (senteo): center
  6. 자기 (jagi): oneself, self
  7. 따라오다 (ttaraoda): to follow
  8. 작성하다 (jakseonghada): to fill out, to write
  9. 대하다 (daehada): to treat, to deal with
  10. 감동하다 (gamdonghada): to be moved, touched
