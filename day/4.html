Day 04: Entertainment 공연과 여가

Learn 10 Korean words about entertainment: 공연 (performance), 용돈 (allowance), 예매하다 (to book/reserve), 마침 (just in time) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 공연 (gongyeon): performance
  2. 용돈 (yongdon): allowance
  3. 예매하다 (yemaehada): to book/reserve
  4. 마침 (machim): just in time, coincidentally
  5. 관객 (gwangaek): audience
  6. 떠들다 (tteodeulda): to make noise, chatter
  7. 집중하다 (jipjunghada): to focus, concentrate
  8. 제대로 (jedaero): properly, correctly
  9. 감상하다 (gamsanghada): to appreciate, enjoy
  10. 아쉽다 (aswipda): to be regrettable, unfortunate
