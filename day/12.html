Day 12: Fatigue & Change 피로와 변화

Learn 10 Korean words about fatigue & change: 변화 (change), 졸리다 (to be sleepy), 하품 (yawn), 피부 (skin) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 변화 (byeonhwa): change, variation
  2. 졸리다 (jollida): to be sleepy
  3. 하품 (hapum): yawn
  4. 피부 (pibu): skin
  5. 여드름 (yeodeureum): pimple, acne
  6. 머리카락 (meorikarak): hair (strand of hair)
  7. 점점 (jeomjeom): gradually, increasingly
  8. 빠지다 (ppajida): to fall out, be missing
  9. 푹 (puk): deeply, soundly, sufficiently
  10. 피로 (piro): fatigue, tiredness
