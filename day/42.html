Day 42: At the laundromat 빨래방에서

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about at the laundromat: 빨래방 (laundromat), 세탁기 (washing machine), 건조기 (clothes dryer), 세제 (laundry detergent) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 빨래방 (ppallaebang): laundromat
  2. 세탁기 (setakgi): washing machine
  3. 건조기 (geonjogi): clothes dryer
  4. 세제 (seje): laundry detergent
  5. 섬유 유연제 (seomyu yuyeonje): fabric softener
  6. 탈수 (talsu): spin-drying (laundry)
  7. 헹구다 (hengguda): to rinse
  8. 널다 (neolda): to hang laundry out to dry
  9. 개다 (gaeda): to fold clothes
  10. 얼룩 (eolluk): stain
