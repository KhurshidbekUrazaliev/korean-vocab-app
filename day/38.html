Day 38: At the cinema 영화관에서

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about at the cinema: 영화관 (movie theater), 상영관 (screening room), 영화표 (movie ticket), 매표소 (ticket booth) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 영화관 (yeonghwagwan): movie theater
  2. 상영관 (sangyeonggwan): screening room
  3. 영화표 (yeonghwapyo): movie ticket
  4. 매표소 (maepyoso): ticket booth
  5. 예고편 (yegopyeon): trailer for a film
  6. 자막 (jamak): subtitles
  7. 감독 (gamdok): director
  8. 배우 (baeu): actor
  9. 줄거리 (julgeori): plot, storyline
  10. 개봉하다 (gaebonghada): to release a film
