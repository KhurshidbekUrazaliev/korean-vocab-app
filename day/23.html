Day 23: Moving day 이사하는 날

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about moving day: 이사 (moving house), 사다리차 (ladder truck), 상자 (box), 테이프 (tape) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 이사 (isa): moving house
  2. 사다리차 (sadaricha): ladder truck
  3. 상자 (sangja): box
  4. 테이프 (teipeu): tape
  5. 포장하다 (pojanghada): to pack or wrap
  6. 옮기다 (omgida): to move something
  7. 나르다 (nareuda): to carry from one place to another
  8. 풀다 (pulda): to unpack or untie
  9. 현관 (hyeongwan): entryway
  10. 열쇠 (yeolsoe): key
