Day 40: Chuseok traditions 추석 풍습

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about chuseok traditions: 추석 (Chuseok), 송편 (half-moon rice cake), 명절 (traditional holiday), 보름달 (full moon) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 추석 (chuseok): Chuseok, the Korean harvest festival
  2. 송편 (songpyeon): half-moon rice cake
  3. 명절 (myeongjeol): traditional holiday
  4. 보름달 (boreumdal): full moon
  5. 차례 (charye): ancestral rite held on a major holiday
  6. 성묘 (seongmyo): visit to an ancestor's grave
  7. 고향 (gohyang): hometown
  8. 친척 (chincheok): relative
  9. 한복 (hanbok): traditional Korean clothing
  10. 보자기 (bojagi): wrapping cloth
