Day 50: Graduation day 졸업하는 날

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about graduation day: 졸업 (graduation), 졸업식 (graduation ceremony), 졸업장 (diploma), 학사모 (graduation cap) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 졸업 (joreop): graduation
  2. 졸업식 (joreopsik): graduation ceremony
  3. 졸업장 (joreopjang): diploma
  4. 학사모 (haksamo): graduation cap
  5. 학위 (hagwi): academic degree
  6. 졸업생 (joreopsaeng): graduate
  7. 동창 (dongchang): former schoolmate
  8. 후배 (hubae): junior schoolmate or colleague
  9. 교가 (gyoga): school song
  10. 꽃다발 (kkotdabal): bouquet
