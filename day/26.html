Day 26: Ordering at a café 카페에서 주문하기

Learn 10 Korean words about ordering at a café: 카페 (café), 메뉴판 (menu), 주문하다 (to order), 커피 (coffee) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 카페 (kape): café
  2. 메뉴판 (menyupan): menu
  3. 주문하다 (jumunhada): to order
  4. 커피 (keopi): coffee
  5. 라테 (rate): latte
  6. 우유 (uyu): milk
  7. 얼음 (eoreum): ice
  8. 빨대 (ppaldae): drinking straw
  9. 머그잔 (meugeujan): mug
  10. 시럽 (sireop): syrup
