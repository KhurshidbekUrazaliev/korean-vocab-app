Day 35: Playing music 악기 연주

Learn 10 Korean words about playing music: 악기 (musical instrument), 연주하다 (to play an instrument), 기타 (guitar), 장구 (Korean hourglass-shaped drum) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 악기 (akgi): musical instrument
  2. 연주하다 (yeonjuhada): to play an instrument
  3. 기타 (gita): guitar
  4. 장구 (janggu): Korean hourglass-shaped drum
  5. 피아노 (piano): piano
  6. 악보 (akbo): sheet music
  7. 박자 (bakja): beat or meter
  8. 음정 (eumjeong): interval between notes
  9. 합주 (hapju): ensemble performance
  10. 조율하다 (joyulhada): to tune an instrument
