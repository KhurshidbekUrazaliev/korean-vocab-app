Day 05: Decisions & Opinions 결정과 의견

Learn 10 Korean words about decisions & opinions: 결정하다 (to decide), 의견 (opinion), 차이 (difference), 모이다 (to gather) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 결정하다 (gyeoljeonghada): to decide
  2. 의견 (uigyeon): opinion
  3. 차이 (chai): difference
  4. 모이다 (moida): to gather
  5. 찬성하다 (chanseonghada): to agree
  6. 반대하다 (bandaehada): to oppose
  7. 충분히 (chungbunhi): sufficiently
  8. 만족하다 (manjokhada): to be satisfied
  9. 방향 (banghyang): direction
  10. 선택하다 (seontaek-hada): to choose
