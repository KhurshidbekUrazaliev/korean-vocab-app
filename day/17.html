Day 17: Asking for directions 길 묻기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about asking for directions: 길 (road or way), 지도 (map), 골목 (alley), 사거리 (four-way intersection) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 길 (gil): road or way
  2. 지도 (jido): map
  3. 골목 (golmok): alley
  4. 사거리 (sageori): four-way intersection
  5. 왼쪽 (oenjjok): left side
  6. 오른쪽 (oreunjjok): right side
  7. 건너편 (geonneopyeon): opposite side
  8. 직진하다 (jikjinhada): to go straight ahead
  9. 건너다 (geonneoda): to cross
  10. 돌다 (dolda): to turn
