Day 34: Art and museums 미술과 박물관

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about art and museums: 미술관 (art museum), 박물관 (museum), 전시회 (exhibition), 작품 (work of art) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 미술관 (misulgwan): art museum
  2. 박물관 (bangmulgwan): museum
  3. 전시회 (jeonsihoe): exhibition
  4. 작품 (jakpum): work of art
  5. 화가 (hwaga): painter
  6. 초상화 (chosanghwa): portrait painting
  7. 풍경화 (punggyeonghwa): landscape painting
  8. 조각상 (jogaksang): statue
  9. 액자 (aekja): picture frame
  10. 관람하다 (gwallamhada): to view an exhibition or performance
