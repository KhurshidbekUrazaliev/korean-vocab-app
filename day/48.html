Day 48: Lost and found 분실물 찾기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about lost and found: 분실물 (lost item), 분실물 센터 (lost-and-found office), 주인 (owner), 맡기다 (to leave in someone's care) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 분실물 (bunsilmul): lost item
  2. 분실물 센터 (bunsilmul senteo): lost-and-found office
  3. 주인 (juin): owner
  4. 맡기다 (matgida): to leave in someone's care
  5. 잃어버리다 (ireobeorida): to lose
  6. 되찾다 (doechatda): to get back something lost
  7. 우산 (usan): umbrella
  8. 목도리 (mokdori): scarf
  9. 장갑 (janggap): gloves
  10. 열쇠고리 (yeolsoegori): key ring
