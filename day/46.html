Day 46: Checking into a hotel 호텔에 체크인하기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about checking into a hotel: 호텔 (hotel), 객실 (guest room), 숙박 (overnight stay), 로비 (lobby) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 호텔 (hotel): hotel
  2. 객실 (gaeksil): guest room
  3. 숙박 (sukbak): overnight stay
  4. 로비 (robi): lobby
  5. 조식 (josik): breakfast (at a hotel)
  6. 룸서비스 (rumseobiseu): room service
  7. 프런트 (peureonteu): front desk
  8. 체크인 (chekeuin): check-in
  9. 체크아웃 (chekeuaut): checkout
  10. 카드 키 (kadeu ki): key card
