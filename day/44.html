Day 44: Caring for a pet 반려동물 돌보기

Learn 10 Korean words about caring for a pet: 반려동물 (companion animal), 강아지 (puppy), 고양이 (cat), 사료 (animal feed) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 반려동물 (ballyeodongmul): companion animal
  2. 강아지 (gangaji): puppy
  3. 고양이 (goyangi): cat
  4. 사료 (saryo): animal feed, pet food
  5. 간식 (gansik): snack or treat
  6. 목줄 (mokjul): dog leash
  7. 꼬리 (kkori): tail
  8. 발바닥 (balbadak): sole of a foot, underside of a paw
  9. 쓰다듬다 (sseudadeumda): to stroke or pet
  10. 짖다 (jitda): to bark
