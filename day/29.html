Day 29: Exercise and fitness 운동과 체력

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about exercise and fitness: 운동하다 (to exercise), 체력 (physical strength), 근육 (muscle), 아령 (dumbbell) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 운동하다 (undonghada): to exercise
  2. 체력 (cheryeok): physical strength
  3. 근육 (geunyuk): muscle
  4. 아령 (aryeong): dumbbell
  5. 줄넘기 (julleomgi): jumping rope
  6. 스트레칭 (seuteureching): stretching
  7. 달리기 (dalligi): running
  8. 팔 굽혀 펴기 (pal gupyeo pyeogi): push-ups
  9. 땀 (ttam): sweat
  10. 숨이 차다 (sumi chada): to be out of breath
