Day 36: Photography 사진 촬영

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about photography: 사진기 (camera), 렌즈 (lens), 필름 (film for a camera), 삼각대 (tripod) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 사진기 (sajingi): camera
  2. 렌즈 (renjeu): lens
  3. 필름 (pilleum): film for a camera
  4. 삼각대 (samgakdae): tripod
  5. 셔터 (syeoteo): shutter
  6. 초점 (chojeom): focus
  7. 구도 (gudo): composition of a picture
  8. 배경 (baegyeong): background
  9. 촬영하다 (chwaryeonghada): to shoot a photo or video
  10. 인화하다 (inhwahada): to print a photograph
