Day 19: Clothes shopping 옷 사기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about clothes shopping: 옷 (clothes), 바지 (trousers), 치마 (skirt), 셔츠 (shirt) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 옷 (ot): clothes
  2. 바지 (baji): trousers
  3. 치마 (chima): skirt
  4. 셔츠 (syeocheu): shirt
  5. 외투 (oetu): overcoat
  6. 소매 (somae): sleeve
  7. 탈의실 (taruisil): changing room
  8. 거울 (geoul): mirror
  9. 크기 (keugi): size
  10. 입어 보다 (ibeo boda): to try on clothing
