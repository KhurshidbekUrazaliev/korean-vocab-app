Day 24: Cooking at home 집에서 요리하기

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about cooking at home: 요리하다 (to cook), 재료 (ingredient), 도마 (cutting board), 칼 (knife) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 요리하다 (yorihada): to cook
  2. 재료 (jaeryo): ingredient
  3. 도마 (doma): cutting board
  4. 칼 (kal): knife
  5. 냄비 (naembi): cooking pot
  6. 두부 (dubu): tofu
  7. 대파 (daepa): large green onion
  8. 썰다 (sseolda): to slice
  9. 끓이다 (kkeurida): to boil a liquid
  10. 간장 (ganjang): soy sauce
