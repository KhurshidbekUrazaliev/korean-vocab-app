Day 31: At the beach 해변에서

Dictionary-reviewed

Learn 10 Korean words about at the beach: 해변 (beach), 모래 (sand), 파도 (wave), 바닷물 (seawater) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 해변 (haebyeon): beach
  2. 모래 (morae): sand
  3. 파도 (pado): wave
  4. 바닷물 (badanmul): seawater
  5. 조개껍데기 (jogaekkeopdegi): seashell
  6. 수영복 (suyeongbok): swimsuit
  7. 파라솔 (parasol): beach umbrella
  8. 밀물 (milmul): incoming tide
  9. 썰물 (sseolmul): outgoing tide
  10. 물놀이 (mullori): playing in the water
