Day 15: Actions & Objects 집안일

Learn 10 Korean words about actions & objects: 올려놓다 (to put up), 쓸다 (to sweep), 걸레 (rag), 밟다 (to step on) and more, with audio and four practice modes.

  1. 올려놓다 (ollyeonota): to put up, place on top
  2. 쓸다 (sseulda): to sweep
  3. 걸레 (geolle): rag, mop
  4. 밟다 (bapda): to step on, tread on
  5. 엉덩이 (eongdeongi): buttocks, bottom
  6. 묻다 (mutda): to get dirty, stain
  7. 탁자 (takja): table, desk
  8. 차다 (chada): to kick
  9. 빠져 (ppajyeo): fall out, escape (빠지다)
  10. 부러지다 (bureojida): to break, snap
