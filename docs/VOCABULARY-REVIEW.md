# Vocabulary review for Days 16–50

On November 28, 2026, all 350 entries were checked for Hangul spelling and spacing, Revised Romanization, and the English meaning in the illustrated lesson. The review changed 15 entries. All 35 lessons retain their generated-content provenance and word IDs, so existing flashcards, quizzes and study history still refer to the same words.

The November 19 edition of the National Institute of Korean Language’s Korean Basic Dictionary yielded 333 whole-headword matches after normalizing spacing for lookup. We compared the original spacing, dictionary pronunciations and definitions with the lesson text. For the remaining 17 expressions and ambiguous matches, we checked standard dictionary entries, usage examples or the words making up the phrase. For example, the recycling lesson uses 폐지 in its 廢紙 sense, meaning discarded paper.
